Is it for one parent or both?

Both can play on one card. If they'd like their own scores on the leaderboard, get a card each. They can also tap their card on a friend's phone, so friends can play today's Hotseat too, free.

What is the price and validity of the card?

The card is priced at ₹799 ₹365 (early bird offer, first 2000 cards only) including GST for a year of the daily Hotseat challenge from the day the user starts using the card. The card is not renewable and there are no recurring expenses.

Which phones work, and what if my parents' phone has no NFC?

Tap-to-open needs a compatible NFC phone; support varies by model. If tapping does not work, use the QR code or link instead. An internet connection is needed for the daily challenges. Our team can help with setup on WhatsApp.

Do they need to be tech savvy?

Not at all. If they can use WhatsApp, they can use Fullhouse. We put their login on the card before it ships, so there's no sign-up or password: they download the app once, tap the card, and they're in. After that, one tap opens the day's challenge - no menus, no navigation. We're on WhatsApp if they'd like help.

What if my parents don't use it?

Request a full refund within 30 days of delivery, no questions asked and no physical return required. Request your refund through the website.

When will the card be delivered?

The first batch ships on 1 November 2026, with delivery expected 5–7 days after dispatch. Shipping is free across India. This is a dispatch date, not a guaranteed Diwali arrival date. Delivery details.

What is the size of the Fullhouse Card?

Same as a credit card - it slips easily into a wallet or sits on a side table.

Is this a board game?

No. The Fullhouse Hotseat is a tap-to-play card - tapping it on their phone opens the day's challenge in the Fullhouse app. New quizzes, games, and memory prompts arrive every single day for 365 days.

Is Fullhouse a good gift for parents or grandparents?

It suits someone who enjoys quizzes, familiar stories and a small daily activity on their phone. You can play a few challenges together at the start. It is not an age test, and nobody needs to be competitive or get every answer right.

Can they play in Hindi or Gujarati?

Daily challenges are available in Hindi, Gujarati and English. Try our free Hindi paheliyan or Gujarati ukhana with your family.

Can I gift it from abroad to parents in India?

We deliver across India. You can arrange a gift for someone living in India; checkout shows the payment methods available to you. If your overseas payment method is not accepted, contact us on WhatsApp for help before ordering. International delivery is not available yet.

Is Fullhouse a medical product or a treatment for memory loss?

No. Fullhouse is for entertainment and daily mental engagement. It does not diagnose, prevent or treat dementia or other medical conditions. Read our guide to cognitive reserve for Indian families for the research context.
